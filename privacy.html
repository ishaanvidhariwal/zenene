Privacy

LAST UPDATED 2026

Zenene is a small, independent project. This page explains plainly what we collect and why — no legal jargon, no surprises.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email, password (handled entirely by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password), and the display name you choose.
  • Journal, mood, and gratitude entries you write, so they can be shown back to you across sessions and devices.
  • Resources you save from Explore.
  • Messages you send Vera, so a response can be generated. If you're logged in, Vera may read your recent journal and mood entries to make her responses more relevant to you.

How it's used

Your data is used only to run Zenene for you: showing your own entries back to you, and letting Vera respond with context. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Where it lives

Account and entry data is stored with Supabase. Messages you send Vera are processed by a Cloudflare Worker we run, which calls Anthropic's Claude API to generate her replies. Neither provider is permitted to use your data to train their own models on our behalf.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your own journal, mood, and gratitude entries at any time from within the app. To delete your account and all associated data entirely, email us at zenene.com@gmail.com and we'll take care of it.

Not a substitute for care

Zenene, including Vera, is a reflective and supportive tool — not a licensed therapist or a crisis service. If you're in immediate danger or crisis, please contact a local emergency service or crisis line.

Questions

Reach out any time at zenene.com@gmail.com.
